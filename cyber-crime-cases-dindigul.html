Cyber Crime Cases in Dindigul

The Dindigul office of R.S.M. Balachander handles cyber crime matters, including online financial fraud, cyber complaints and bank account freezes or liens linked to cyber investigations.

What to do after online financial fraud

Report the transaction promptly to your bank and the national cybercrime helpline at 1930. Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to file or track a complaint. Keep the acknowledgement number, transaction reference, date, amount and relevant messages or screenshots.

What details to ask for if an account is frozen

Ask the bank in writing whether the restriction covers the whole account or a specific amount, and request the reason, date and any available complaint or police reference. Keep the bank’s reply, transaction records, and any police communication. The appropriate next step depends on who requested the restriction, its scope and the available records.

Which records are useful for a cyber complaint

Keep a chronological record of events and preserve original emails, messages, payment receipts and bank alerts. Include the transaction IDs and relevant account or wallet references in reports to the bank or official authorities. Keep passwords, PINs and one-time codes private.
