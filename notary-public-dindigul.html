When you may need a Notary Public

A Notary Public may be requested for certain affidavits, declarations, copies, signatures, agreements or other documents depending on the purpose and the authority receiving them. Requirements are not the same for every document.

What to carry

Original document, where applicable
Required photocopies
Valid photo identification
Supporting records requested for the document

Do not sign a document in advance if the receiving procedure requires the signature to be witnessed. Call the office first when unsure.
