Accident Cases in Dindigul

The Dindigul office of R.S.M. Balachander handles accident-related legal matters. This page covers records commonly relevant after a road accident and explains how motor accident compensation claims are considered.

Records to keep after a road accident

Keep any available police report or FIR, vehicle and insurance details, photographs, and witness contact information. If there was an injury, retain the medical records, discharge summary, prescriptions and bills. Keep copies of communications with the insurer and a dated account of events.

What is an MCOP?

A Motor Accident Claims Original Petition is a petition for compensation following a motor vehicle accident. Sections 165 and 166 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 address the Claims Tribunal and applications for compensation. The claim and the appropriate forum depend on the facts and the applicable law.

Where to check MCOP court information in Dindigul

The Dindigul District Court directory lists a Special Sub Court for MCOP Cases in the Dindigul Combined Court Building. Use the official directory for court details and refer to the court named on any notice or existing case documents.
