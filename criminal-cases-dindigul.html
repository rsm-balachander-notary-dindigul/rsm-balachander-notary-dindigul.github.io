Criminal Cases in Dindigul

The Dindigul office of R.S.M. Balachander handles criminal matters, including bail, anticipatory bail, police notices and criminal court proceedings.

Bail and anticipatory bail

Bail concerns release from custody, subject to the applicable procedure and any conditions. Anticipatory bail is sought before a possible arrest. Section 482 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 deals with applications to the Court of Session or High Court where a person apprehends arrest for a non-bailable offence. Eligibility, exceptions and conditions depend on the applicable law and facts.

What to keep ready after a police notice

Keep the complete notice, the police station name, any FIR or case reference available, and the date and time shown on the notice. A dated summary of events and copies of any previous court orders help explain the stage of the matter. Preserve the original documents and messages.

How to find court information in Dindigul

The Dindigul District Court website lists the district’s courts and their official contact details. Use the court name and case number from the relevant notice or order when looking up a proceeding.
