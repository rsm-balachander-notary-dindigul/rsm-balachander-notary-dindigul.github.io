Notary document attestation

Some institutions ask for notarized copies or notarized signatures. The exact process depends on the type of document and the organization where it will be submitted.

Before you visit

Keep the original document, copies and valid identification available. If the document is for a government office, bank, educational institution, overseas use or another authority, tell the office the exact purpose so the appropriate requirement can be checked.

Notary attestation is different from apostille, embassy attestation and some departmental certifications. Confirm what the receiving authority specifically asks for.
