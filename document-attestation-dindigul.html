What is a certified true copy?

For certificate copy attestation, the notary compares a photocopy with the original. Ask the organisation receiving your document whether it requires a notarised true copy and whether it has any specific instructions.

When a notarised copy may be needed

An educational institution, employer, bank or government office may request a copy of a certificate or other document. Ask the institution which document it needs and whether it accepts a notarised copy, self-attestation or certification by the issuing authority.

What to bring for copy attestation

  • The original document and a clear photocopy.
  • Valid photo identification, if required.
  • Any written instructions from the receiving institution.
Notarial copy attestation, apostille and embassy attestation are separate requirements. For documents intended for use abroad, check the process specified by the receiving authority.
